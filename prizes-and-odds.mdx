Every machine has one prize table, fixed at launch. A prize is a multiple of the coins your own pack bought. So a large pack and a small pack have the same odds. A pack that costs 50 times more has prizes about 50 times larger.

## The app's table

The Gabox app launches every machine with this table:

| Row | Chance | Prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Common | 80% | 0.5126× your pack coins |
| Rare | 15% | 2× |
| Epic | 4% | 3.5× |
| Mythic | 1% | 10× |

On average this table pays 0.9499× your pack coins. The row names are app labels. The program only knows multipliers and tickets.

## The program's rules

A machine created through the SDK can use another table. The program accepts a table only when:

- it has at most 8 rows;
- the tickets of all rows add up to 65,536;
- no row pays more than 10× the pack coins;
- the average payout is at most 1× the pack coins;
- every row pays at least one raw coin unit for the pack.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Multiplier | Paid as basis points of the pack coins. `10,000` is 1×; `100,000` is 10×. |
| Tickets | Equal chances for that row. All rows add up to 65,536. |
| Prize | Pack coins × multiplier / 10,000, rounded down, in raw coin units. |
| Chance | `tickets / 65,536`. |

## Caps

A prize can never be larger than what the vault can pay:

```text
prize = min(row prize, free coins + your pack coins)
```

The free coins are the vault coins that other draws have not reserved. The app shows each row's **payable** amount before you sign. You sign `min_maximum`, the smallest top prize you accept.

When the vault is thin, the top rows are capped. Their chances do not change. New packs and donations add free coins over time. Large wins remove them. So the top prize can be capped for a while after a big win.

Example: your pack bought 1,000,000 coins, and the vault has 3,000,000 free coins. The Mythic row names 10,000,000 coins, but it can pay at most 4,000,000.

## Not a promise of value

These rules count coins, not dollars. A prize in coins can be worth less than the pack price, because the coin price can move. Read [Seed and jackpot](/seed-and-jackpot) and [How prizes stay funded](/always-funded).
