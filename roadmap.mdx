## Rollout

| Phase | Ships |
|---|---|
| **v0** | Platform-seeded Tokenized assets and NFTs pools · backed positions · harmonic-mean pricing · MagicBlock VRF draws · keep or sell back · crown tithe |
| **v1** | Public deposits at scale · value-tiered pools (the whale lane) · pull feed and crown leaderboard · fee-split tuning |
| **v2+** | Permissionless pool creation on top of the unified engine · optional token |

## The token question

One option is a token (LP seed, short emissions, fee buyback and burn) to bootstrap both sides of the market. The other is monetizing purely on the surcharge. A token adds mercenary liquidity but also regulatory surface, and the default value-weighted fee split already rewards deposit size without one. **Undecided.**

## Open questions

| Question | The tension |
|---|---|
| Surcharge % and its exact split | Crown vs protocol vs depositor shares are tunable; defaults are 15% surcharge, 5% crown, ~35/65 protocol/depositors |
| Value-weighted vs equal-split depositor fees | Value-weighted needs no token; equal-split needs a token to reward size. This choice largely decides the token question |
| Token: yes or no | Bootstrap speed vs regulatory surface |
| Backing denomination | SOL or USDC |
| Keep vs sell-back settlement accounting | Exact handling of the depositor's backing at settlement |
| Depositor exit of an un-drawn position | Withdraw anytime, or a cooldown |
| Pool composition | Do NFT and tokenized-asset pools mix; are crown parameters per-pool |

## The one metric

**Repeat-pull rate per wallet in week one.** Same as every version of this design: if buyers chain-open, the flywheel exists. Deep backed supply and the crown-tithe grail bait are optimization on top of that, not a substitute for it.
