Everything runs on one unified pool engine. Categories and value tiers are *views* over that engine, not separate systems: the same backed-position primitive, pricing formula, and draw mechanics everywhere.

## Curated category pools

At launch the platform seeds two category pools, then opens them to public deposits:

| Pool | Contents |
|---|---|
| Tokenized assets | Tokenized trading cards and other tokenized collectibles (Collector Crypt and similar) |
| NFTs | Solana NFT collections |

Every pool is whitelisted by collection: only curated, verified assets can enter. Curation does three things the v1 permissionless design could not: it concentrates supply instead of fragmenting it across a thousand micro-machines, keeps discovery legible (a buyer knows what each pool contains), and quality-gates what enters.

## Value tiers: the whale lane

Within a category, pools can be banded by value, so a roughly $30 commons pool and a multi-thousand-dollar grails pool coexist:

| Tier | Ticket ballpark | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Commons | ~$30 | High-frequency, chain-opening buyers |
| Mid | ~$100s | Collectors |
| Grails | ~$2,000+ | Whales |

The whale lane is only safe because of the backing model: in a vault design, a whale tier is dangerous, since one big cash-out can drain the shared buyback fund. Here every buyback in the whale tier is collateralized by its own depositor's backing, so **the thing that made big packs dangerous in v2 simply does not exist**.

## What a pool page shows

Verifiability is the aesthetic; the number is always exposed:

- Live EV, ticket price, position count, and TVL.
- The honest odds table: value tier to draw probability.
- The grail, visible, with its 1-in-N odds shown plainly.
- The [crown board](/the-crown): who holds each of the five ranks and what each rank is earning.

No casino kitsch. The energy comes from real onchain state, the grail is still in the pool, the board is contested, not manufactured hype.

<Note>
Whether NFT and tokenized-asset pools can mix is an open product question. Fee rates and windows are per-pool parameters, so tiers can differ where it makes sense; the board's rank shares are protocol constants.
</Note>
