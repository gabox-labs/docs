A gift pays for one pack, of one size, on one machine, for one wallet. Anyone can send a gift to any wallet.

## What a gift is

- A gift is one pack of one size. A gift for a pack on one machine only opens on that machine.
- The gift holds its USDC until it is opened. The pack is bought at the moment of opening, not when the gift is sent.
- Only the wallet you name can open the gift. It opens once.
- Opening a gift works like opening a paid pack: same odds, same reveal, and the prize goes to the receiver.

## How to send a gift

1. Open the machine and select "Gift a pack".
2. Pick a pack size. The largest pack size is available only after the coin graduates, the same rule as a normal purchase.
3. Enter the wallet address you want to send it to.
4. Select "Send gift" and sign. You pay the pack price in USDC, plus a small amount of SOL for network costs.

## How to open a gift

1. Go to the machine the gift is for, or find it from your profile.
2. Look for the gift card. It names the sender and the pack size.
3. Select "Open gift" and sign.
4. The pack opens like any other pack. You pay a small amount of SOL for network costs, and the prize goes to you.

## Good to know

- A gift has no expiry and no cancel. The sender cannot take it back.
- A gift cannot move to another wallet. Only the wallet it was sent to can open it.
- On a machine priced in another token, such as SOL, the gift's USDC is swapped into that token when it is opened.
- Your gifts, both received and sent, show on your profile. This list is public, like the rest of a profile.
