The **vault** holds the machine's prize coins. The seed, donations and every pack's bought coins go in. Only prize payments take coins out, through the program's normal instructions. There is no creator withdrawal.

```text
free coins   = vault balance − reserved coins
pack's cap   = free coins + the pack's own coins
reservation  = min(full top prize, pack's cap)
```

## One purchase, one reservation

In the same transaction, a purchase:

1. adds its bought coins to the vault;
2. takes a snapshot of the free coins, before its own coins;
3. reserves the most it can pay;
4. asks for randomness.

The program keeps this rule at all times: `vault balance ≥ all reservations`. An open draw reserves its most possible prize. A settled but owed draw reserves exactly what it owes. One draw can never spend another draw's reservation.

When the draw settles, its reservation is released. The prize is paid, or it stays reserved as owed until a claim.

## Caps

Every prize row is capped at `free coins + the pack's own coins`. When the vault is thin, the top rows pay less than their full multiplier. Their chances do not change. The buyer signs `min_maximum`, so a purchase fails if the top prize fell below what the buyer saw.

A [Mega Pack](/mega-pack) draw follows the same rules. While it is open, its reservation also lowers the caps that other buyers on that machine see.

## The table limit

At launch, the prize table's average payout, before any cap, must be at most one pack's coins. This limits the table. The reservations and caps protect each real purchase.

None of these rules protect the coin's market price or promise a dollar value.
