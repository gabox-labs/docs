The **vault** holds the machine's prize coins. The seed, donations and every pack's bought coins go in. Only prize payments take coins out, through the program's normal instructions. There is no creator withdrawal.

```text
free coins   = vault balance − reserved coins
pack's cap   = free coins + the pack's own coins
```

## One purchase, one reservation

In the same transaction, a purchase:

1. adds its bought coins to the vault;
2. takes a snapshot of the free coins, before its own coins;
3. reserves its own coins;
4. asks for randomness.

The program keeps this rule at all times: `vault balance ≥ all reservations`. An open draw reserves only its own pack's coins. So packs that other buyers open at the same time do not lower your offer, and they do not make your purchase fail. A settled but owed draw reserves exactly what it owes. One draw can never spend another draw's reservation.

When the draw settles, its reservation is released. The prize is paid, or it stays reserved as owed until a claim.

## Caps

Every prize row is capped at `free coins + the pack's own coins`. When the vault is thin, the top rows pay less than their full multiplier. Their chances do not change. The app shows the capped amounts before you buy: they are the most the vault can pay at that moment.

The cap is checked again when your draw settles. If another win settles first and uses free coins, a large prize can pay less than the amount you saw. A win never pays less than your pack's own coins, and the smallest prize is never cut. The odds and the prize table do not change.

The buyer signs `min_maximum`, so a purchase fails if the top prize fell below what the buyer saw before the purchase landed.

The [Mega Pack](/mega-pack) never uses the prize vault.

## The table limit

At launch, the prize table's average payout, before any cap, must be at most one pack's coins. This limits the table. The reservations and caps protect each real purchase.

None of these rules protect the coin's market price or promise a dollar value.
