---
title: "Risks"
description: "What a pack can and cannot promise"
icon: "triangle-alert"
---

- **Coins, not dollars.** A pack wins coins, not a fixed amount of USDC. The coin can fall in price or lose its market.
- **Average payout.** A valid table pays at most one pack's coins on average. The app's table pays about 95%. The trading fee and the pack fee come out of the price first. So on average a pack is worth less than you paid.
- **Price movement.** The market sets the price. A $10 pack buys fewer coins when the coin trades higher. Your signed limits protect you from a stale quote, but the purchase can then fail.
- **Swap cost.** On a SOL or stock token machine, your USDC is swapped first. That swap has its own price and fees.
- **Selling.** A large prize can move the market when you sell it. A sale pays the trading fee. On a stock token or SOL machine, you receive that token, not USDC.
- **Thin vault.** A top prize can pay less than its full multiplier when free vault coins are low. Your `min_maximum` protects the offer you accepted.
- **Late randomness.** Only the buyer can retry a draw, and nobody retries for you automatically. After the deadline, anyone can settle the pack at its smallest prize.
- **Mega Pack trust.** Gabox's server builds the holder snapshot and the reward list. The program checks the accounting, not the split. Files let anyone check the work afterwards.
- **Mega Pack timing.** Traders can buy before a Mega Pack opens and sell after it. One late draw delays a whole round's rewards.
- **Creator fees.** Creator earnings depend on trading that may never happen.
- **Outside systems.** Meteora, MagicBlock, Jupiter, Irys, Solana, the quote token's issuer, the RPC and the program's upgrade authority can each fail or act. A stock token issuer can pause or freeze its token.

Read [People and rules](/parties-and-rules), [If the draw is late](/if-the-draw-is-late) and [Trust boundaries](/trust-boundaries) before you buy.
