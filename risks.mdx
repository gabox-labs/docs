---
title: "Risks"
description: "What a pack can and cannot promise"
icon: "triangle-alert"
---

- **Coins, not dollars.** A pack wins coins, not a fixed amount of USDC. The coin can fall in price or lose its market.
- **Average payout.** A valid table pays at most one pack's coins on average. The app's default table pays about 96.5%. The trading fee and the pack fee come out of the price first. So on average a pack is worth less than you paid.
- **Price movement.** The market sets the price. A pack buys fewer coins when the coin trades higher. Your signed limits protect you from a stale quote, but the purchase can then fail.
- **Transfer fee.** Every transfer of a new coin costs 1% of the coins, sales and wallet transfers included. Prizes arrive in full, because the machine reserve pays their fee.
- **Selling.** A large prize can move the market when you sell it. A sale pays the trading fee and the transfer fee. On an older machine not quoted in USDC, you receive its quote token, not USDC.
- **Swap cost.** On an older machine not quoted in USDC, your USDC is swapped first. That swap has its own price and fees.
- **Low machine reserve.** A top prize can pay less than its full multiplier when the machine reserve's free coins are low. Your `min_maximum` protects the offer when you buy. If another win settles before yours, a large prize can still pay less than that offer, but a win never pays less than your pack coins, less the transfer fee.
- **Late randomness.** Only the buyer can retry a draw, and nobody retries for you automatically. After the deadline, anyone can settle the pack at its smallest prize.
- **Older coins.** A machine whose coin launched before the transfer fee sells no packs while that coin is on its curve.
- **Creator fees.** Creator earnings depend on trading that may never happen.
- **Outside systems.** Meteora, MagicBlock, Jupiter, Solana, the quote token's issuer, the RPC and program upgrades can each affect you. A stock token issuer can pause or freeze its token.

Read [People and rules](/parties-and-rules), [If the draw is late](/if-the-draw-is-late) and [Trust boundaries](/trust-boundaries) before you buy.
