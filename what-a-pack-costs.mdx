A pack costs its listed USDC price. The pack fee and the market's trading fee are already inside that price. Nothing is added on top, except SOL for the network, rent and randomness.

## What the price pays for

| Part | Rule | Paid to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pack fee | 0.20% of the amount charged | The buyer's referrer, or Gabox. After graduation, with no referrer, the machine's Mega Pack. |
| Trading fee | 1.875% on the curve, 0.94% after graduation, inside the market price | Meteora, Gabox and the creator |
| Coins | Everything else buys coins for the vault | The machine's vault |
| Swap | Only on a machine not quoted in USDC: the USDC to quote token swap | The swap route |
| Network, rent, VRF | Variable costs, paid in SOL | Solana and MagicBlock |

## How the pack fee is taken

The pack fee is inclusive. The program takes it out of the pack's budget before it buys:

```text
market budget = budget − floor(budget × 20 / 10,000)
pack fee      = floor(amount charged × 20 / 10,000)
```

Example: a pack priced at 10 USDC on a USDC machine has a budget of 10,000,000 raw USDC units. The market may spend up to 9,980,000. If it spends all of it, the pack fee is 20,000 units, or 0.02 USDC, and the total is exactly 10 USDC. If the market spends less, for example on the last pack of the curve, the fee is smaller and the rest stays with the buyer.

## Trading fees

Every trade on a Gabox coin pays a trading fee inside the market price. Gabox only accepts market configs with these terms, so every coin pays the same:

| Market | Total | Meteora | Gabox | Creator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bonding curve (DBC) | 1.875% | 0.30% | 1.08% | 0.495% |
| After graduation (DAMM v2) | 0.94% | about 0.15% | about 0.54% | about 0.25% |

- **On the curve.** Meteora keeps 20% of the fee. Of the rest, the creator gets 33% and Gabox 67%. Meteora pays 20% of its own part back to the referral account that the trade names. Gabox names its own account there, so this 0.075% is part of Gabox's 1.08%.
- **After graduation.** Meteora keeps 20% of the pool fee, with the same rebate. The rest goes to the pool's liquidity, which is locked for ever: 67% is Gabox's position and 33% is the creator's.
- All fees are collected in the machine's quote token.

## Fee summary

| Action | Pack fee | Trading fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open a pack on the curve | 0.20% | 1.875% |
| Open a pack after graduation | 0.20% | 0.94% |
| Sell coins | None | 1.875% on the curve, 0.94% after graduation |
| Seed buy at launch | None | 1.875% |

A Mega Pack payout does not trade, so it pays no fee.

## Before and after graduation

Before graduation, the market is the Meteora DBC bonding curve. After it, the market is a Meteora DAMM v2 pool. The pack prices stay the same. The number of coins a pack buys follows the market price.
