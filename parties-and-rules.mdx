A **machine** belongs to one new coin. These are the parties in its life:

| Party | Can do | Cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator | Launch the coin and machine, pay the seed, buy coins for themselves at launch (the dev buy), fund more prize coins and withdraw what they funded, and claim their part of the trading fees from Meteora. The app offers one fixed prize table for each top prize; SDK clients can pass another valid table. | Change the prize table after launch, withdraw the seed, the transfer fees or more than they funded, withdraw while a pack is opening, or earn pack fees on their own machine (except as somebody's referrer). |
| Buyer | Open one pack per purchase, set price and prize limits, keep or sell the coins, retry a late draw, and claim an owed prize. | Change a draw's result or choose its random value. |
| Referrer | Receive 0.20% of the price of every pack a buyer bound to them opens, and claim it. | Bind a buyer to themselves, or change an existing link. |
| Trader | Buy, sell and send the coin anywhere it trades. | Skip the 1% transfer fee, or change its rate. |
| Gabox admin | Register quote tokens for new machines, stop new machines or purchases on a quote, claim Gabox's pack fees, and replace a machine's prize table with one that pays at least as much on average. | Lower a machine's average payout, change the table of a pack already bought, change a coin's transfer fee, or change a machine's config or open draws through the program's instructions. |
| Anyone | Move collected transfer fees into the machine reserve, expire an overdue draw, pay an owed prize into the buyer's own coin account, and close a finished draw. | Redirect another buyer's prize, or send transfer fees anywhere except the coin's own machine reserve. |
| Meteora DBC / DAMM v2 | Price every trade. DBC runs the curve; DAMM v2 runs the pool after graduation. | Choose a prize. |
| MagicBlock VRF | Return random bytes through an authenticated callback. | Change the prize table or redirect a payment. |

The program can be upgraded. New code can change these rules. Check [status](/status-and-addresses) and [trust boundaries](/trust-boundaries).

## Three kinds of fees

The **pack fee** is 2% of the pack price. It is already inside the price. With a referrer, the referrer gets 0.20% of the price and Gabox the rest. With no referrer, all of it goes to Gabox.

The **trading fee** is inside Meteora's price on every trade, buys and sales. Meteora, Gabox and the creator share it.

The **transfer fee** is 1% of every transfer of a new coin. It goes into the coin's machine reserve. See [What a pack costs](/what-a-pack-costs) and [Referrals and earnings](/referrals-and-earnings).
