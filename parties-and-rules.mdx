A **machine** belongs to one new coin. These are the parties in its life:

| Party | Can do | Cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator | Launch the coin and machine, pay the seed, add more prize coins, and claim their part of the trading fees from Meteora. The app uses a fixed prize table; SDK clients can pass another valid table. | Change the prize table after launch, take coins out of the vault, or earn pack fees on their own machine (except as somebody's referrer). |
| Buyer | Open one pack per purchase, set price and prize limits, keep or sell the coins, retry a late draw, and claim an owed prize. | Change a draw's result or choose its random value. |
| Referrer | Receive the whole 0.20% pack fee of every buyer bound to them, and claim it. | Bind a buyer to themselves, or change an existing link. |
| Holder | After graduation, share each Mega Pack prize by balance, and claim it. | Trigger a Mega Pack opening. |
| Gabox admin | Register quote tokens for new machines, stop new machines or purchases on a quote, set the Mega Pack target and operator, and claim Gabox's pack fees. | Change an existing machine's prize table, config or open draws through the program's instructions. |
| Mega operator | A Gabox server wallet. It opens Mega Packs, retries their draws, and publishes the holders' reward lists. | Change settings, upgrade the program, or move Mega funds outside these steps. |
| Anyone | Add prize coins, expire an overdue draw, pay an owed prize into the buyer's own coin account, and close a finished draw. | Redirect another buyer's prize. |
| Meteora DBC / DAMM v2 | Price every trade. DBC runs the curve; DAMM v2 runs the pool after graduation. | Choose a prize. |
| MagicBlock VRF | Return random bytes through an authenticated callback. | Change the prize table or redirect a payment. |

The admin key also holds the program's upgrade authority in the last recorded deployments. New code can change these rules. Check [status](/status-and-addresses) and [trust boundaries](/trust-boundaries).

## Two kinds of fees

The **pack fee** is 0.20% of the pack price. It is already inside the price. It goes to the buyer's referrer, or to Gabox when there is no referrer. After graduation, a fee with no referrer goes to the machine's [Mega Pack](/mega-pack) instead, when the Mega Pack is on for that quote token.

The **trading fee** is inside Meteora's price on every trade, buys and sales. Meteora, Gabox and the creator share it. See [What a pack costs](/what-a-pack-costs) and [Referrals and earnings](/referrals-and-earnings).
