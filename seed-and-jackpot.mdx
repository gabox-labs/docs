The Gabox app launches machines with a fixed prize table. Its top row is **10×**, or 10,000,000 coin tokens for one pack. The program accepts other valid tables with a top row up to **20×**, including machines created through the SDK. The prize a buyer can actually receive is limited by free tokens in the vault.

## The seed at launch

The creator buys the seed on the coin's DBC curve, straight into the prize vault. For the app's 10× table, the **mandatory seed is two packs**: 2,000,000 tokens. The first pack adds another 1,000,000 tokens, so its top row can initially pay **3×**. The 10× row keeps its fixed odds, but its payable amount is capped until more tokens are free.

The app's **Starting liquidity** choices add *backup seed* on top of those two mandatory packs:

| App choice | Extra seed | Total seed at launch | First pack's payable top prize |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No seed | 0 packs | 2 packs | 3× |
| Seed 1 jackpot | 7 packs | 9 packs | 10× |
| Seed 2 jackpots | 16 packs | 18 packs | 10×, with enough inventory for two consecutive 10× wins |

“No seed” means **no extra seed**. The creator still buys the mandatory two packs. All seed is irrevocable prize inventory; the creator cannot withdraw it. Later purchases and donations can increase free inventory, while large wins and pending reservations can reduce it. The app shows the current payable amounts before a buyer signs.

## The program rule

For any valid prize table, the program computes `mandatory seed = min(full top prize, 3 packs) − 1 pack`, then adds the creator's chosen extra seed. The first pack brings the remaining one pack. A custom 20× machine therefore also starts with a 3× payable top row if it has only the mandatory seed. To make 20× payable on its first pack, it needs 17 extra packs beyond the two mandatory packs.

The app can list a valid 20× machine created outside its launch form. Its marketplace card shows the **currently payable** top prize, which may be 3× while the vault is thin, rather than the table's 20× headline.

The creator signs a quote asset cost cap and a native SOL debit cap for the launch seed buy. That buy pays no Gabox pack fee. See [Launch a machine](/launch-a-machine) and [Prizes and odds](/prizes-and-odds).
