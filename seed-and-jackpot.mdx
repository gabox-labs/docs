The seed is the creator's first donation of prize tokens. It is bought on the DBC bonding curve and placed directly in the vault.

## The rule

The mandatory seed supports a 3× top prize on the first pack, or the full top prize when the largest multiplier is below 3×:

```text
mandatory seed = min(jackpot amount, 3 packs) − 1 pack
```

For a 1,000,000 token pack:

| Top prize | Mandatory seed |
| --- | --- |
| 1× | 0 packs |
| 2× | 1 pack |
| 3× | 2 packs |
| 5× | 2 packs |
| 20× | 2 packs |

The largest multiplier may be at most 20× and at least 1×. A creator may add `extra_seed_tokens` in the same launch trade. Extra seed is visible capacity, not withdrawable balance.

The first buyer contributes one pack to the vault. The mandatory seed adds enough for the first 3× top prize, or the smaller full top prize. Later packs and donations refill inventory. Gabox reserves each pending maximum before selling the next pack.

The displayed top prize can be capped after large wins. The buyer signs a floor for the first pack's maximum. A capped result still settles from the frozen table and available inventory.

The creator signs quote and native debit caps for the seed buy. The seed buy pays no 0.75% pack fee. See [Launch a machine](/launch-a-machine) and [Always funded](/always-funded).
