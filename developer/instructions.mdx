The Gabox program has 24 instructions. This page groups them by task. The argument order is the program's order. The IDL (interface description) and the SDK's `@gabox-labs/sdk/generated` entry point carry the exact account lists.

All quote amounts are raw units of the machine's quote token. All coin amounts are raw units of the coin, which has 9 decimals.

## Launch and registry

| Instruction | Signer | Arguments | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `initialize_pool` | Creator | `tiers`, `max_seed_quote_in`, `max_seed_native_debit`, `extra_seed_tokens` | Opens a machine for a coin that DBC just launched on the quote's registered config, before any trade. Buys the mandatory seed plus the extra seed into the vault. The seed buy pays no pack fee. |
| `fund_prizes` | Any coin holder | `amount` | Adds coins to the prize vault. Nobody can take them back. |
| `register_quote` | Platform admin | `reference_valuation` | Lets new machines use a quote token with one checked DBC config. Also creates Gabox's own `Earnings` record for the quote. |
| `set_quote_enabled` | Platform admin | `enabled` | Stops or allows new machines on a quote. Existing machines keep trading. |
| `set_quote_purchases` | Platform admin | `on` | Pauses or resumes purchases on every machine of a quote. Pending draws still settle. |

A launch is one transaction: DBC creates the coin and its curve, the hook creates the coin's account list, and `initialize_pool` opens the machine. The program refuses a coin without the Gabox transfer hook, a coin from another config, and a curve that already traded.

## Purchase and sale

| Instruction | Signer | Arguments | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `buy_fixed_pack` | Buyer | `pack_tier`, `max_quote_budget`, `nonce`, `min_net_tokens`, `min_maximum`, `max_native_debit`, `referrer` | Buys one pack, reserves its largest prize, and requests one VRF result. |
| `buy_pack` | Buyer | `quote_amount`, `nonce`, `min_net_tokens`, `min_maximum`, `max_native_debit`, `referrer` | Retired. It always fails with `LegacyPurchaseDisabled`. |
| `sell_tokens` | Seller | `amount`, `min_quote_output`, `max_native_debit` | Sells coins through the machine's market: DBC before graduation, DAMM v2 after. A sale pays no pack fee. |

### The limits a buyer signs

`buy_fixed_pack` checks every limit inside the same transaction. If one fails, nothing happens.

| Limit | What it caps |
| --- | --- |
| `max_quote_budget` | The most quote the pack may cost, with the 0.20% pack fee included. |
| `min_net_tokens` | The fewest coins the vault must receive from the market. |
| `min_maximum` | The lowest top prize the buyer accepts, after the vault cap. |
| `max_native_debit` | SOL the handler spends: market account rent, the VRF fee, and a new referral link or earnings record. |

Anchor creates the `Draw`, the `WalletActivity`, the buyer's coin account and the fee vault before the handler runs. Their rent is outside `max_native_debit`. The network fee is outside it too.

The quote comes from one of two sources:

- **The buyer's quote ATA.** The account must hold at least `max_quote_budget`. The program spends at most that amount. The unused part stays in the account.
- **A temporary account** at `create_with_seed(buyer, "gbx" + nonce, quote token program)`. The client creates and funds it in the same transaction, for example with a USDC swap. The program spends up to `max_quote_budget`, returns the rest to the buyer, and closes the account.

`pack_tier` must be 0 to 4. On the DBC curve, tier 4 fails with `PackTierLocked`.

<Note>
The program checks the tier number only. It does not check the dollar value of `max_quote_budget`, and it uses no price oracle. The SDK and the app turn the catalog price ($10 to $1,000) into a quote budget. A direct caller can sign any budget under any tier.
</Note>

The whole pack fee goes to one `Earnings` record. With a referrer, that is the referrer's record. Without one, it is the platform admin's record. On a graduated machine whose quote has `mega_on`, it is the machine's Mega funding record.

## Draws and prizes

| Instruction | Signer | Arguments | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `deliver_draw` | MagicBlock VRF identity | `randomness`, `seq` | The VRF callback. Requires the draw's `seq`. Settles the pack and pays the prize. If the buyer's coin account cannot receive, it records the prize as owed. |
| `retry_draw` | The draw's purchaser | `max_vrf_debit` | Requests new randomness after 300 slots, up to 3 attempts in total. The purchaser pays the VRF fee. |
| `expire_draw` | Anyone | none | From 216,000 slots after purchase, settles the pack at its smallest prize row. It never refunds the purchase. |
| `claim_prize` | Anyone | none | Pays an owed prize to the purchaser's coin ATA. The caller pays the rent if the ATA is new. |
| `claim_prize_to` | The draw's purchaser | none | Pays an owed prize to any coin account the purchaser owns. |
| `close_draw` | Anyone | none | Closes a settled draw that owes and reserves nothing. The rent goes back to the purchaser. It refuses a Mega draw. |

The callback signer is the PDA `["identity", Gabox program ID]` under the MagicBlock VRF program. Delivery stops at the expiry slot, and expiry starts there, so the two never compete.

## Earnings

| Instruction | Signer | Arguments | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `claim_earnings` | The credited wallet | none | Pays every unclaimed pack fee of that wallet in one quote token. If the fee vault is short, the rest stays owed. |

## Mega Pack

| Instruction | Signer | Arguments | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `init_mega_config` | Platform admin | `target_usdc_raw`, `operator` | Creates `MegaConfig` once, at `version` 1. |
| `update_mega_config` | Platform admin | `target_usdc_raw`, `operator` | Changes the target or the operator. Adds 1 to `version`. |
| `set_quote_mega` | Platform admin | `on` | Switches Mega funding on or off for one quote. Existing credit stays. |
| `open_mega` | Operator | `round`, `draw_index`, `config_version`, `budget`, `min_tokens`, `min_maximum`, `max_native_debit`, `expiry_slot`, `snapshot_slot`, `snapshot_hash`, `snapshot_id` | Buys one pack on a graduated machine with its Mega credit. The prize goes to the reward escrow. The opening pays no pack fee. |
| `seal_mega_round` | Operator | `round`, `final_draw_index` | Ends the openings of the latest round. With no pending draw, it also funds the round. |
| `retry_mega_draw` | Operator | `max_vrf_debit` | Retries a Mega draw's randomness, with the same limits as `retry_draw`. |
| `finish_mega_draw` | Anyone | none | Adds one settled Mega draw's reward to the round, then closes the draw. The last finish after the seal funds the round. |
| `publish_mega_root` | Operator | `root`, `version`, `through_round`, `allocated`, `distribution_id` | Publishes the Merkle root of the cumulative distribution. Works exactly once for each completed round. |
| `claim_mega` | The claimant | `cumulative`, `proof` | Pays the claimant everything the current root gives them on this machine, minus what they claimed before. |

`open_mega` checks more than an ordinary purchase:

- The signer is `MegaConfig.operator`, and `config_version` matches.
- `expiry_slot` is no more than 150 slots ahead. The snapshot is no more than 1,500 slots old.
- `draw_index` equals `next_draw`, so a repeated transaction cannot open a second draw.
- A new round needs every earlier round to be funded and in the published root.
- The budget comes only from the machine's Mega funding record. The operator's quote account must end at its starting balance.

The program trusts the operator for the snapshot, the allocation and the dollar value of a non-USDC budget. It enforces the accounting: `claimed <= allocated <= funded`, and no wallet can claim more than its cumulative amount in the root.
