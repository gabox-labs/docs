Gabox is an onchain gacha for brand new Solana coins. A creator launches a coin and its **machine** together. A buyer opens a **pack** at a fixed USDC price. MagicBlock's verifiable randomness picks the prize from a fixed table, and the prize is paid in the coin.

## The short version

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What is a pack? | A fixed price in USDC: $10, $50, $200, $500 or $1,000. The price buys coins on the market, and the prize is a multiple of those coins. |
| What does it cost? | The pack price. It already includes the 0.20% pack fee and the market's trading fee. Network fees, rent and the randomness fee are paid in SOL. |
| Where is the market? | A Meteora bonding curve until graduation, then a Meteora DAMM v2 pool. |
| Who picks the prize? | MagicBlock VRF, against the machine's fixed prize table. |
| How big can a prize be? | Up to 10× the coins your pack bought, if the vault holds enough. |
| Can the creator take the prizes back? | No. Nobody can withdraw the prize vault. |
| What is the Mega Pack? | After graduation, pack fees fill a shared pack. It opens on its own, and the coin's holders share the prize. |
| Is it an investment? | No. On average a pack pays back at most the coins it bought. The app's table pays about 95%. The coin price can also fall. |

## Who does what

- **Creator:** launches the coin and its machine, pays the seed, and earns part of the trading fees.
- **Buyer:** pays a fixed pack price, signs price and prize limits, and gets the prize.
- **Referrer:** gets the whole 0.20% pack fee of every buyer they referred.
- **Holder:** holds the coin after graduation and shares each Mega Pack prize.
- **Gabox:** runs the program, holds the vault, checks every reservation and pays prizes.
- **Meteora:** runs the market and charges a trading fee inside each trade.
- **MagicBlock:** supplies the randomness.

## Safety boundaries

Each machine belongs to one brand new coin. Its prize table cannot change after launch. Every open draw reserves the most it can pay, so one draw cannot spend another draw's prize. A valid table pays at most one pack's coins on average.
