Gabox is an onchain gacha for brand new Solana coins. A creator launches a coin and a machine together. A buyer opens one to five packs. Each pack is 1,000,000 tokens, and MagicBlock VRF chooses the prize from a fixed table.

## The short version

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What is a pack? | 1,000,000 tokens, or 0.1% of a 1,000,000,000 token supply. |
| What does it cost? | The coin's market price for one pack, plus a 0.75% Gabox pack fee and normal network, rent, and VRF costs. |
| Where is the market? | Meteora DBC until graduation, then Meteora DAMM v2. |
| Who chooses the prize? | MagicBlock VRF, using the creator's immutable prize table. |
| Can the creator withdraw prizes? | No. Extra funding is a donation. |
| Is it an investment? | No. Average token payout is capped at one pack, and the coin price can move. |

## Who does what

- **Creator:** launches the coin, fixes the prize table, pays the seed, and receives creator earnings.
- **Buyer:** pays the market price and pack fee, signs cost and prize floors, and receives the draw result.
- **Referrer:** may earn 0.50% of the measured market charge after a buyer binds them.
- **Gabox:** holds the vault, checks reservations, requests VRF, and pays or records prizes.
- **Meteora:** supplies the market and charges its 1.25% venue fee inside each trade.
- **MagicBlock:** supplies the randomness callback.

## Safety boundaries

The machine is bound to one brand new Token-2022 coin. Its table, pack size, and fee rules are fixed at launch. Every pending draw reserves its maximum possible payment. The table's uncapped expected payout cannot exceed one pack.
