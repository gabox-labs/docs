Once the draw settles, the prize coins are yours. There is nothing to claim on the normal path: they go straight to your wallet.

## Keep

Do nothing. The coins stay in your coin account. If the prize was recorded as owed, claim it first. See [If the draw is late](/if-the-draw-is-late).

After graduation, holding the coin also counts for the [Mega Pack](/mega-pack).

## Sell

Sign `sell_tokens` with the amount of coins and your minimum output.

1. The app quotes the current market: the DBC curve before graduation, the DAMM v2 pool after it.
2. You sign `min_quote_output`, the least quote tokens you accept. The program checks it after the trade.
3. The program sells through the machine's own market.
4. The market takes its trading fee: 1.875% on the curve, 0.94% after graduation. Gabox takes no pack fee on a sale.

You receive the machine's quote token. On a SOL machine the proceeds arrive as SOL. On a USDC machine you receive USDC. On a stock token machine you receive that stock token. The app does not swap it back to USDC.

You can sell part of your coins. A large prize can move the market price more than a small one, so check the quote before you sign. There is no Gabox buyback and no guaranteed price.

## At graduation

When the curve completes, the market moves from DBC to DAMM v2. Your coins stay yours. While Meteora moves the liquidity, the app cannot sell for a short time. After that, you can trade the coin anywhere it is listed, not only through Gabox.
