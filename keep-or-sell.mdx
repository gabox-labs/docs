Once the draw settles, the prize is yours. Keeping it costs no Gabox fee.

## Keep

Do nothing. Tokens remain in your coin account. If delivery was recorded as owed, use `claim_prize` or `claim_prize_to` first.

## Sell

Sign `sell_tokens` with the amount and your minimum quote output.

1. The app quotes the current DBC or DAMM v2 market.
2. You sign `min_quote_output`. The venue checks this floor before delivery.
3. The program sells through the official market.
4. The venue takes its 1.25% trade fee. Gabox takes no pack fee on a sale.

A sale may be partial. A jackpot can move the market more than a common prize, so its received quote depends on the live market and amount sold. There is no Gabox buyback or guaranteed price.

At graduation, the market changes from DBC to DAMM v2. The tokens remain yours, and the program continues to enforce the market and minimum-output checks.
